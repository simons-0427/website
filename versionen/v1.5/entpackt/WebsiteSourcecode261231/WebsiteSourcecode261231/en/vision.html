The bigger goal

02

Education and direction

Where business
meets technology.

I know that I want to move towards industrial engineering after school. The combination of technical execution and economic logic appeals to me, so I am currently focusing on a strong school-leaving qualification.

The degree should be a tool. I want to understand economic relationships and eventually execute projects myself. My goal is to assess ideas and know how to build, test and improve them in practice.

To do that, I want to develop a broad range of skills, including technical knowledge as well as rhetoric, communication and negotiation. I want to keep learning in all of these areas.

03

Financial freedom

Independence.

Financial freedom is one of my goals. To me, success means security, independence and the ability to shape my life more deliberately. I want to choose which projects I pursue, how I work and where I invest my time.

That is why business and finance interest me so much. I want to understand how money, companies, markets and decisions work, then make these topics more tangible through my own tools, data analyses and dashboards.

"

Success is not a status symbol to me.
Success means the freedom to choose.

04

Technology

Technology as a tool
for concrete problems.

At the same time, I am expanding my skills in software development and modern technologies. C and C++ were my first programming languages. I used them to build small games and discovered how exciting it is to turn an idea into a real program. To publish these projects on the web, I learned HTML, CSS and some JavaScript.

Today I am especially interested in artificial intelligence, data analysis, blockchain and web applications. To me, these are tools for solving economic and technical problems in a useful way. My website grows with each new project and serves as a presentation and archive of what I learn and build.

🤖 Artificial intelligence 📊 Data analysis 🔗 Blockchain 🌐 Web applications ⚙️ Software development 💡 Strategic thinking
05

Projects

Improve by
building.

I like working through projects because building is where I learn the most. Small applications, web projects, simple games, analyses and dashboards help me turn theory into practice. When I develop something myself, I understand much faster where the real problems are.

I make these projects available publicly so that others can benefit from a tool, visualisation or small project too. One grand idea is not the point. I care more about getting steadily better and gaining practical experience through many smaller projects.

I analyse data and build my own tools to understand relationships better and make complex topics easier for myself and others to follow. In the long term, I can see myself building smaller projects or a company alongside my studies and later career. Carefully developed products with genuine value matter more to me than rapid growth.

06

How I work

Understand first.
Then build.

My way of working combines technical execution with strategic thinking. I want to understand how a system works, where its weaknesses are and how to recreate or improve it myself. That combination of understanding and execution is central to me.

I am mainly driven by the desire for financial independence, knowledge and freedom. I want to create the option to decide for myself over the long term which projects I pursue and how I spend my time. That is why I try to gain practical experience early.
